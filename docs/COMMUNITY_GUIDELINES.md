# Community guidelines

Use a pseudonym if you prefer. Respect a person's choice not to answer, reveal information or continue a conversation. Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, exploit minors, share private information without permission, send spam or distribute illegal content.

You can block an account, leave a group, decline an invitation and end Unmasked Mode. A report submits only the selected message text you explicitly approve, your reason and relevant metadata to your self-hosting operator. This version records reports but does not include a staffed service or moderator console. Before onboarding public users, the operator must publish a reachable safety contact, response policy and appeal process.

Do not rely on Unmasked for emergency assistance or guaranteed confidential communication. This version has no end-to-end encryption. No biometric verification or public stranger matching is required.
